- Scenario `source.commit` is a subject commit. Because updating a scenario creates a later commit, local HEAD can differ from `source.commit`; this is not remote-equivalence proof.
- `mcp_flutter` still reports existing skill metadata/source warnings during `make check-contracts`; they are warnings, not gate failures.
- Broad H5 remains unclaimed until repeated evidence promotes reusable capabilities across producer and consumer contexts with owner, risk, redaction, and validation.
